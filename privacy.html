Privacy Policy

Worbe Budget · Last updated: June 2026

Worbe Budget ("the app", "we", "us") is designed to keep your financial information private and under your control. This policy explains what we collect, how it is used, and your choices.

1. Using the app without an account

You can use Worbe Budget without creating an account. In this mode, all data you enter — budgets, transactions, income, deductibles, invoices, and goals — is stored only on your device. We do not receive or have access to it.

2. Optional account & sync

If you choose to create an account (via Sign in with Apple or email and password), we store the data you enter in the app on our secure cloud backend so it can sync across your devices and, if you choose, be shared with family members you invite. For account creation we process your email address (or the identifier provided by Sign in with Apple) solely to authenticate you and sync your data.

3. What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell your personal or financial data.
  • We do not use your financial data for advertising.
  • We do not share your data with third parties except the infrastructure providers required to operate the sync service, acting on our behalf.

4. Family Access

If you invite a family member, the budget data in your account becomes visible to the accounts you explicitly invite. You can revoke access at any time in Settings → Account → Family Access.

5. Data retention & deletion

Data stored only on your device is removed when you delete the app. If you have an account, you can permanently delete your account and all associated synced data at any time directly in the app, in Settings → Account → Delete Account. Deletion is immediate and irreversible. You may also request deletion by emailing us at the address below.

6. Children

Worbe Budget is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email support@worbe.org.
