Privacy Policy

Worbe Budget · Last updated: June 2026

Worbe Budget ("the app", "we", "us") is designed to keep your financial information private and under your control.

1. Using the app without an account

You can use Worbe Budget without creating an account. All data is stored only on your device. We do not receive or have access to it.

2. Optional account & sync

If you create an account (via Sign in with Apple or email and password), we store your data on our secure cloud backend to sync across your devices. We process your email address solely to authenticate you and sync your data.

3. What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell your personal or financial data.
  • We do not use your financial data for advertising.
  • We do not share your data with third parties except infrastructure providers required to operate the sync service.

4. Family Access

If you invite a family member, your budget data becomes visible to invited accounts. You can revoke access at any time in Settings → Account → Family Access.

5. Data retention & deletion

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data in Settings → Account → Delete Account. Deletion is immediate and irreversible.

6. Children

Worbe Budget is not directed to children under 13.

7. Contact

Questions? Email support@worbe.org
