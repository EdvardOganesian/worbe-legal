Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?
No. Tap "Continue without an account" on the welcome screen to use every feature on your device. Create an account anytime to back up, sync across devices, and share lists with your partner.

How do I share a list with my partner?
Create an account, then open Settings → Family and invite them by email. Once they sign in with that email, you'll both see the shared lists. Every item shows who added or changed it.

What are list categories?
When you create a list you pick a type — Shopping, Travel, Places, Watch & Read, Events, Expenses, a simple Checklist, or Ideas — and the list shows the right fields for it (prices and links, days and times, a map location, and so on).

How do split expenses work?
In an Expenses list, add who's splitting, then log each expense with who paid and how to split it. The list shows a live balance of who owes whom. Swipe an expense to mark it settled.

Can I keep a list just for myself?
Yes. When creating a list, turn off "Share with partner" to make it private — only you can see it.

Is my data private?
Yes. Your data is stored on your device, and if you create an account it is synced securely so you can use Wishare across your devices. We never sell your data or use it for advertising. See our Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my account?
Open Settings → Account → Delete Account. Deletion is immediate and removes all your synced data.
