Privacy Policy

Worbe Wishare · Last updated: July 2026

Worbe Wishare ("the app", "we", "us") is a shared wish-list app for couples and families. This policy explains what we collect, how it is used, and your choices.

1. Using the app without an account

You can use Worbe Wishare without creating an account. In this mode, all data you enter — lists, items, links, prices, notes, and expenses — is stored only on your device. We do not receive or have access to it.

2. Optional account & sync

If you choose to create an account (via Sign in with Apple or email and password), we store the data you enter in the app on our secure cloud backend so it can sync across your devices and, if you choose, be shared with a partner or family members you invite. For account creation we process your email address (or the identifier provided by Sign in with Apple) and an optional display name, solely to authenticate you, show who added or changed shared items, and sync your data.

3. What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not share your data with third parties except the infrastructure providers required to operate the sync service, acting on our behalf.

4. Sharing with a partner or family

If you invite someone by email, the lists you share become visible to the accounts you explicitly invite. Lists you mark as private stay visible only to you. You can revoke a member's access at any time in Settings → Family.

5. Data retention & deletion

Data stored only on your device is removed when you delete the app. If you have an account, you can permanently delete your account and all associated synced data at any time directly in the app, in Settings → Account → Delete Account. Deletion is immediate and irreversible. You may also request deletion by emailing us at the address below.

6. Children

Worbe Wishare is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email support@worbe.org.
