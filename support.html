Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?
No. Tap "Continue without an account" on the welcome screen to use every feature on your device. Create an account anytime to back up and sync across devices.

Is my financial data private?
Yes. Your data is stored on your device. If you create an account, it is synced securely so you can use Worbe across your iPhone and iPad. We never sell your data. See our Privacy Policy.

How do I estimate my taxes?
Open Settings → Tax, set your tax rate, and mark which income sources are taxable. Worbe shows estimated taxes per month and per year automatically.

How do I export deductibles for my accountant?
Open the Business → Deductible tab and use the export button to generate a PDF summary.

Can my partner use the same budget?
Yes — open Settings → Account → Family Access and invite them by email.

How do I hide my balances?
Turn on Settings → General → Privacy → Hide Balances, or tap the eye icon on the Dashboard.
